EmpireMUD Soundpack

Press F1 in the game window at any time to open this file.


GETTING STARTED

Unzip the soundpack, open the empiremud-soundpack folder, and run MUSHclient.exe. Everything is already set up, so there's nothing else to install.

When the game asks for your account name, type it, then your password, then pick a character. You go straight into the game.

If Windows Defender's Controlled Folder Access is on, don't keep the soundpack in Documents or on the Desktop, because Defender stops it from updating there. Put it somewhere like C:\empiremud-soundpack instead.


UPDATES

The soundpack updates itself. A few seconds after you open it, it checks for new sounds and changes, downloads them, and tells you what changed. To check again without restarting, type spupdate. Updates need Windows 10 or 11.


VOLUME

Type voltoggle to pick which volume to change: global, ambients, MSP (fighting, spells and abilities), battle music, or music.
Type volup or voldown to change the one you picked by 10.
Type volumes to hear all five.
Turning a volume down to 0 mutes it. Turning it back up starts the ambience again. Your volumes are remembered.

Keys for volume:
F11 turns the global volume down by 5, and F12 turns it up by 5.
Ctrl+F10 turns the ambience down by 5, and Ctrl+F11 turns it up by 5.
Ctrl+F12 turns all sounds off, and pressing it again turns them back on.


SHORTCUT KEYS

F1 opens this file.
Shift+F1 lists the mobs in the room with you.
Alt+P says your health, and Alt+B says your blood if you're a vampire.
Alt+K looks around.
Alt+D sits, Alt+E stands, and Alt+X sleeps.

Moving with Alt keys, laid out like a compass on the right side of the keyboard:
Alt+U northwest, Alt+I north, Alt+O northeast.
Alt+J west, Alt+L east.
Alt+M southwest, Alt+Comma south, Alt+Period southeast.
Alt+R up, Alt+F down.


SHORT COMMANDS

hp or health: your health.
mana, mn or mp: your mana.
move or mv: your movement.
blood or bl: your blood, for vampires.
loot: gets everything from a corpse.
winv: your warehouse inventory.
wstore and an item: stores it in the warehouse.
wretrieve and an item: takes it out of the warehouse.
widen and an item: identifies an item in the warehouse.
qi and a quest: quest info. qf and a quest: finishes a quest.
mat and a material: summons that material.
ooc and a message: talks on the ooc channel. newbie and a message: talks on the newbie channel.
ss: stops all sounds that are playing.


SETTINGS YOU CAN TURN ON OR OFF

coords off hides the map coordinates in room names, and coords on shows them again. With coordinates hidden, room ambience doesn't play.
wonderland on, artus on and autocook on turn on automatic help for some adventures and for cooking. Type the same thing with off to turn it off again.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If the keyboard seems frozen, press Escape.
If sounds stop, press Ctrl+F12 twice, or close MUSHclient and open it again.
For anything else, type support in the game and tell the staff what happened.


HELPING WITH THE SOUNDPACK

To add sounds, triggers or keys, read CONTRIBUTING.txt in the main soundpack folder.
