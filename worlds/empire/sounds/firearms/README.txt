FIREARM SOUNDS NEEDED

Put these 8 sound files in this folder (worlds\empire\sounds\firearms).
They must be .ogg files with these exact names.

1. revolver-fire.ogg
   Revolver shot. Plays on any revolver hit, miss, or kill (lines starting
   with BANG!).

2. handgun-fire.ogg
   Handgun shot. Plays on any handgun hit, miss, or kill (lines starting
   with BLAM!).

3. empty.ogg
   Empty gun click. Plays when you try to shoot with no rounds left.

4. load-rounds.ogg
   Loading rounds into the revolver (the reload command).

5. fill-magazine.ogg
   Filling a magazine with 9mm rounds (reload magazine).

6. magazine-in.ogg
   Putting a magazine into the handgun (reload while holding the handgun).

7. magazine-out.ogg
   Ejecting the magazine from the handgun (unload handgun).

8. unload.ogg
   Taking rounds out of the revolver or out of a magazine
   (unload revolver, unload magazine).

When they're all in, double-click "make release.bat" in
C:\Users\Owner\muds\soundpack-release and upload the folder it tells you.
